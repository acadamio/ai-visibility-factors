## What is it?

Forum and community presence is genuine discussion of a person, organization, product, or problem in places such as Reddit, Quora, or specialist communities. It includes independent user experiences and useful participation by knowledgeable representatives. A forum thread may reveal questions and problems absent from polished product pages, but posts are not inherently verified. This factor concerns the substance of public discussions, not the number of promotional comments an organization places. The relevance of a community depends on its audience and the questions being asked. [Google's discussion-forum documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/discussion-forum) and [Reddit Rules](https://redditinc.com/policies/reddit-rules).

## Impact details

**Impact: Medium where community experiences are central to the user's question.** Google recognizes discussion pages as a distinct Search content type and supports forum markup to identify first-hand perspectives for Search features. Its SEO guide also lists community engagement as one way useful content can be discovered. A relevant, indexed thread may therefore become a source for an AI answer about practical experiences or recurring issues. This does not mean posting in a forum directly increases a company's ranking or recommendations. Negative experiences, incorrect claims, and inaccessible threads may produce very different outcomes. [Google's discussion-forum documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/discussion-forum), [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), and [AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## Proof & consensus details

**Proof: Low. Consensus: Mixed** for AI citation impact. Google documents how some forum pages can be understood and shown in Search, but it does not publish a formula converting community participation into AI mentions. Bing values original, clear, verifiable content yet warns against artificial social promotion. Forum posts can be insightful first-hand reports or anonymous speculation; neither a vote total nor a popular thread proves a claim. Reddit's rules call for authentic participation and prohibit spam and content manipulation. Treat community evidence as a source to investigate, not as a guaranteed authority signal. [Google's discussion-forum documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/discussion-forum), [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a), and [Reddit Rules](https://redditinc.com/policies/reddit-rules).

## Recommendation

Listen for recurring questions, misconceptions, and product problems in relevant communities. When a representative participates, identify the affiliation when appropriate, answer the actual question, and follow each community's rules. Use public feedback to improve documentation or the product rather than attempting to overwrite criticism with promotional replies. Link to a first-party page only when it genuinely helps. Do not create fake users, coordinate votes, or mass-post identical claims. On a forum you operate, preserve readable threads and accurate author or date information; use forum markup only when it matches the visible discussion.

## AI platforms

**Google AI Overviews and AI Mode** can use Search-eligible pages, including relevant discussions, without requiring the organization to post in forums.

**Bing and Copilot** may retrieve public discussions but publish no community-post count rule.

**ChatGPT** gives no universal formula for Reddit, Quora, or specialist forums. When an AI answer cites a thread, check whether it accurately distinguishes a user's anecdote from a verified fact or the company's official position. [Google's AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a), and [OpenAI's publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq).

## Audit instructions

1. Identify a few communities where customers discuss the relevant product, service, or problem and find current threads.
2. Read a sample for recurring questions, factual errors, and genuine experiences; note source dates and whether company representatives are clearly identified.
3. Fix related first-party information or answer where appropriate under community rules. Test AI answers separately for misattributed or overgeneralized forum claims.
