## What is it?

Completeness means a page covers the important information needed for its purpose. Depth means it explains material that a reader must understand, rather than stopping at a generic definition. The right scope depends on the query: a simple factual page can be short, while a buying guide may need criteria, limits, examples, and trade-offs. This factor does not reward length for its own sake. Google's helpful-content guidance asks whether a page gives a substantial, complete description and leaves the reader able to achieve their goal.

## Impact details

**Impact: High when missing information prevents a useful answer.** A page that omits eligibility, process, price conditions, methodology, or significant exceptions may be less useful for the questions people actually ask. Google says its AI features draw on core Search quality and ranking systems and emphasizes helpful, non-commodity content. Bing says content should fully satisfy intent and advises deepening subject coverage where grounding queries reveal gaps. More depth can support a broader set of relevant passages, but it does not guarantee that the page will be retrieved or cited. Unrelated sections can dilute focus. [Google's AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a), and [Bing's AI Performance guide](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview).

## Proof & consensus details

**Proof: Medium. Consensus: Strong** on substantive coverage as a quality criterion, with no universal depth threshold. Google explicitly rejects writing to a preferred word count and asks whether people would need to search again. Bing links focused, complete content with grounding reliability. These are primary platform statements, not controlled experiments assigning a citation increase to a given number of sections. A comprehensive page can still be inaccurate or inaccessible; quality depends on which information is present, how well it is supported, and whether it fits the intended question. [Google's helpful-content guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a).

## Recommendation

Define the page's job and list the decisions or questions a reader must resolve. Add missing facts, examples, evidence, and limitations that materially help that job. Explain terms where a new reader would otherwise lose the thread. For reviews or comparisons, cover the criteria that affect a real choice and say why. Avoid padding with generic history or repeated headings merely to appear comprehensive. When a related subtopic deserves its own page, give the reader a clear internal link rather than forcing every subject into one URL. Revisit the page as products, policies, or evidence change.

## AI platforms

**Google AI Overviews and AI Mode** inherit Search quality and eligibility requirements; useful depth can make a page relevant to more specific questions [Google's AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

**Bing and Copilot** explicitly advise focused, complete pages and show grounding-query information in Bing Webmaster Tools [Bing's AI Performance guide](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview).

**ChatGPT** does not publish a page-length target or comprehensive-coverage rule. Judge whether the content answers representative queries, and observe actual AI citations independently [OpenAI's publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq).

**Claude:** Anthropic’s Research evaluation includes whether an answer covers the requested aspects. This concerns answer quality, not a publisher word-count requirement; the practical inference is to supply the details needed for the question rather than maximize page length. [Anthropic’s Research system explanation](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system).

**Perplexity:** Deep Research assembles reports from many sources. A complete answer can therefore combine pages; one publisher need not cover the whole topic. The documentation gives no minimum page length for inclusion. [Perplexity Deep Research](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/products/deep-research).

**Gemini:** Deep Research creates a plan and analyzes multiple sources. This supports checking coverage against the research question, but does not establish that longer or more comprehensive individual pages receive preferential visibility. [Gemini Deep Research guidance](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111?hl=en).

## Audit instructions

1. Write down the page's main purpose and the key questions a reader must resolve to finish that task.
2. Check the page for each answer, including evidence and material limits. Note gaps that would make a reader search again.
3. Fill the important gaps and remove filler. Ask a reader to complete the task using the revised page, then monitor representative Search and AI answers separately.
