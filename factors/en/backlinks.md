## What is it?

A backlink is a link from another website to a page on your site. Links can help crawlers discover a URL and give readers a path to its source. Their usefulness depends on context: a relevant editorial link from a trusted publication differs from an automated directory link or a paid placement created to manipulate rankings. This factor concerns independent external links, not a site's own internal navigation. Google says it uses links for page discovery and as a relevance signal; Bing also describes external linking as support for discovery and authority evaluation.

## Impact details

**Impact: Medium.** A relevant backlink can introduce a page to a search crawler and provide context about why another publisher considers it worth referencing. Bing explicitly connects natural external links to authority and grounding eligibility, while Google documents links in its ordinary Search systems. Since Google AI Overviews and AI Mode draw supporting pages from Search, this creates an indirect path to AI visibility. It does not mean that a link guarantees ranking, citation, or recommendation; the linked content must still be accessible, relevant, and useful for a particular question. [Google's link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable), [AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), and [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a).

## Proof & consensus details

**Proof: Medium. Consensus: Strong** for ordinary search discovery and evaluation, with weaker direct evidence for AI citations. Google and Bing both document roles for links, and Bing offers a backlink report. Neither publishes a universal number or authority score that converts a backlink into an AI answer appearance. Search systems can discount manipulative links. Both Google and Bing warn against buying links, artificial exchanges, and other schemes intended to inflate authority. A high link count alone is therefore a poor quality measure. [Google's spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a), and [Bing's Backlinks report](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/backlinks-c3a334e8).

## Recommendation

Publish source material others have a reason to cite, such as original evidence, useful tools, clear reference pages, or expert analysis. When an independent publication has already referred to your work, make it easy to link to the most relevant stable URL. Review linking pages for topic fit, genuine editorial context, working destinations, and misleading anchor text. Fix broken or redirected target URLs that discard useful references. Avoid purchasing ranking links or placing keyword-stuffed guest links. Measure referral traffic and actual citations separately from link counts.

## AI platforms

**Google AI Overviews and AI Mode** use Search-eligible pages, so link effects may flow through ordinary crawling and relevance systems; Google does not promise an AI citation for any link. [Google's AI feature guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

**Bing and Copilot** expressly include relevant external links among discovery and authority signals for grounding eligibility. [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a)

**ChatGPT** publishes crawler access guidance but no backlink scoring formula. Judge observed answer citations rather than treating backlink totals as a direct AI visibility metric. [OpenAI's publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq)

## Audit instructions

1. Export a sample of referring pages from a backlink report and identify which important pages they link to.
2. Open the referring pages. Check relevance, editorial context, link placement, and whether the destination works; flag obvious paid or automated schemes.
3. Repair broken destinations and prioritize content worth genuine references. Track AI citations separately from link counts or third-party authority scores.
